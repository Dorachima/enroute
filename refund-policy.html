Refund & Cancellation Policy

EnRoute helps you manage booking cancellations and refunds in line with supplier policies. We do not guarantee specific refund amounts unless stated by the service provider.

Customer Cancellations

If you cancel a booking, your refund will depend on the individual supplier’s rules. Contact EnRoute support to begin the cancellation process.

Supplier Cancellations

If a supplier cancels or changes your booking, EnRoute will inform you and assist with available alternatives or refund guidance.

Booking Changes

Changes to travel plans are handled according to the supplier’s terms. Additional fees may apply for rebooking or itinerary updates.

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed once the supplier approves them. EnRoute will work with payment providers as needed, but processing times depend on the supplier and payment method.
