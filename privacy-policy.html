Privacy Policy

EnRoute collects and uses customer information to provide booking requests, travel support, and service communication. We take privacy seriously and keep your data safe.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details such as name, email, and phone number.
  • Travel details including destination, dates, and service preferences.
  • Account data and booking requests.
  • Payment-related information shared with secure payment providers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process travel requests and booking enquiries.
  • To send booking updates, confirmations and customer support messages.
  • To improve our services and deliver better travel experiences.

Data Sharing

We only share booking details with partners that help deliver your travel services, such as bus operators, airlines, hotels, and transfer providers. We do not sell your personal information.

Security

EnRoute uses standard security practices to keep your information protected. Payment data is processed by trusted external gateways and should not be stored in insecure front-end code.
