Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about EnRoute travel planning.

Learn how to book flights, hotels, transfers, payment options and how to contact support.

How do I book a flight?
Visit the Flight Booking page, select departure, destination, travel dates and passenger details, then submit your request.
Can I book international flights?
Yes. EnRoute supports international flight requests. Submit your travel details and our team will provide available options.
Can I book a hotel through EnRoute?
Yes. Use the Hotel Booking page to submit your stay requirements. We’ll follow up with hotel and apartment options.
Do you offer airport pickup?
Yes. The Airport Transfers page helps you request pickup or drop-off services with vehicle and passenger details.
Can I rent a car?
Yes. Our Car Rentals page collects your pickup, return and vehicle preferences so we can prepare a rental option for you.
How do I pay?
Current booking requests are submitted and arranged through EnRoute. A future payment gateway will be connected for secure online checkout.
How will I receive my booking confirmation?
Booking confirmations and updates are sent to the email or WhatsApp number you provide during the request process.
Can I cancel my booking?
Cancellations depend on the supplier’s rules. Contact EnRoute support through WhatsApp or email for assistance.
Can I contact EnRoute on WhatsApp?
Yes. Use our WhatsApp contact option to ask questions, send travel details or request support anytime.
Do I need an account to make a booking?
An account helps keep your travel requests organized, but you can also submit a booking enquiry without signing in.

Still Have Questions?

Contact our team for help with your booking, service request or corporate travel coordination.
